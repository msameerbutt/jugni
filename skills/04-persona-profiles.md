# Persona profiles

Ten to start with. This list is **expected to grow and change** — an agent
observes patterns across real trips, proposes an addition or edit, a human
Engineer reviews and locks it (`06-skill-maintenance.md`). No separate process
for this Skill.

Each profile is a need or style axis, never an identity label. Religion-driven
needs stay in scope under a need-based name — profile 1 is the example. The
point is that each profile is useful to anyone who shares the need, regardless
of why they have it.

---

**1. Dietary/practice-conscious**
Halal, kosher, vegetarian/vegan, prayer or observance spaces, fasting-period timing.
*Ask:* which requirements, how strict, any fasting dates inside the trip.
*Emphasise:* certified/verified restaurants and grocery chains, prayer spaces and
their hours, fasting-period sunrise/sunset times, phrase for asking about
ingredients in the local language.

**2. Nightlife-focused**
Bars, clubs, late-night food, safety-at-night info.
*Ask:* going out alone or in a group, music preferences, latest night expected out.
*Emphasise:* which districts are alive on which nights, last train and night-bus
times, whether taxis need a local app, cover charges and door norms, late-night food.

**3. Adventure/outdoor**
Hiking, permits, gear, physical difficulty.
*Ask:* target activities, fitness and experience level, gear brought vs. rented,
anything needing booking months ahead.
*Emphasise:* permit systems and deadlines, season and daylight windows, trailheads
reachable by public transport, rental shops, mountain-weather and rescue numbers.

**4. Slow/cultural**
Museums, history, pace, fewer stops per day.
*Ask:* museum-heavy or wander-heavy, guided or self-directed, closure days that matter.
*Emphasise:* closure days and free evenings, city passes worth it at their pace,
advance-booking-only sites, walkable historic quarters.

**5. Luxury/comfort**
Higher-end stays, minimal logistics friction.
*Ask:* transfer preferences, dining reservations to arrange, lounge access.
*Emphasise:* private transfer options, reservation lead times, concierge-worth
requests, quieter arrival routes.

**6. Budget/backpacker**
Hostels, transit passes, cost-per-day tracking.
*Ask:* dorm or private, target daily spend, rail pass vs. per-leg.
*Emphasise:* realistic cost-per-day, transit passes and break-even points,
free-entry days, cheap-eats districts, left-luggage, tap-water drinkability.

**7. Family with kids**
Kid-friendly pacing, nap/meal timing, safety.
*Ask:* ages, nap and meal rhythm, pram or carrier, must-avoids.
*Emphasise:* pram-friendly routes and lift-equipped stations, changing facilities,
kid-priced or free entry, early dinner options, pharmacy and paediatric care.
Treat children's ages as sensitive data (spec §6).

**8. Accessibility needs**
Mobility, sensory, medical access considerations.
*Ask:* the specific needs and what usually goes wrong for them when travelling.
*Emphasise:* step-free stations with verification dates, accessible-entrance
details rather than "accessible ✓", quiet hours at busy sites, pharmacy and
equipment-repair locations, medication rules at the border.
Baseline accessibility of the app itself is a requirement for every trip, not
this profile (spec §8) — this profile is about the *destination* information.

**9. Solo traveller**
Safety info, meeting-people options, single-friendly costs.
*Ask:* first time solo here, comfort with nightlife alone, check-in contact.
*Emphasise:* single-supplement realities, social hostels and walking tours,
areas to avoid after dark with a reason attached, local emergency number,
scam patterns that specifically target solo travellers.

**10. Wellness/retreat**
Spas, quiet, minimal itinerary density.
*Ask:* target density (things per day), specific practices, quiet-accommodation priority.
*Emphasise:* bath and spa etiquette and booking rules, quiet districts, early-morning
options, one-thing-a-day itineraries.

---

## Adding a profile

Propose it when a real trip shows a need the ten do not cover — with the
questions it triggers and the destination emphasis it implies, or it is a label
rather than a profile. Then `06-skill-maintenance.md`.
